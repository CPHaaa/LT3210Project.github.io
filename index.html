Music Taste &
Social Identity

Introduction

Music has become an integral part of human lives, as it started to develop from simple melodies from the combination of clapping, drumming, and varying types of singing(Tumbale, 2025) to the level when an ordinary person is able to generate melodies through gadgets without leaving home. Furthermore, music serves as a powerful tool that mirrors our personalities, as it is closely connected to our identities: various music genres can be associated with an individual’s cultural background, values, behaviours and ideologies. In addition, it can reflect how we perceive ourselves and others in a society (Nagy, 2025). As our group is interested in the relationship between psychological factors and music tastes, we decided to examine the relationship between music tastes and personality traits.



Purpose

We aim to explore the connections between music taste, personality & social life by collecting data from LLM through some profiles of university students. The data obtained from this survey will be used to create this interactive website, which will provide visualisation and analysis of these connections.
