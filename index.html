Music Taste &
Social Identity

Intro

We are students working on a group research project for LT3201: Electronic Publishing course.



Purpose

We aim to to explore the connections between music taste, personality & social life by collecting data from LLM through some profiles of university students.The data obtained from this survey will be used to create this interactive website, which will provide visualization and analysis of this connections.
